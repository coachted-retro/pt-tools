Booking the Scan — Objection Playbook

Never call it a "consultation" — say "body composition scan" or "baseline scan." Always end with an assumptive close (a choice of two times), never an open yes/no question. Getting a tentative time on the books beats no time every time.

"I don't have time for it."
"Totally get it — it's actually just 15 minutes, and it's already included in your membership. We do it for everyone so our trainers know where you're starting from. Does Tuesday morning or Thursday after work fit better?"
"I don't want to schedule it."
"No pressure at all — this isn't a sales thing, it's just a scan and a quick chat about your goals. Let's just get something tentative on the calendar so it's there — you can always move it. Morning or evening usually work best for you?"
"I'm good, thanks anyway."
Usually a habitual brush-off, not a real no — interrupt the pattern with curiosity instead of pushing: "Fair enough — quick question though, have you ever actually had your body composition measured? Most people are surprised by what they find out. Since it's already part of your membership, want to just grab a spot for it?"
General rules
Frame it as standard for every new member, not something targeted at them. Offer two specific times, never "would you like to." If they still decline, don't push — log it and set a soft follow-up for 1-2 weeks out rather than repeating the ask on the spot.
