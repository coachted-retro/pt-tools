Your home base: Command Center ▾

Command Center is the hub — every tool you use links out from here. Bookmark it, start every day here.

  • Client Management — view, search, and manage every client and member. PT Clients are split into Men's and Women's Programs, each with its own trainer filter so you can pull up "just Marin's clients" or "just Roman's clients" directly. Generate Report gives you a coach-grouped CSV export per program.
  • Coaches Hub — see every coach's client count at a glance, click into any coach to see their actual client list.
  • Portal Access Manager — grant a client their login (creates their account, sends them a temporary password).
  • Staff Setup — add employees, assign roles, grant PIN access for staff login.
Adding people to the system ▾

Every "add new" form — clients, staff, prospects — checks for similar existing names as you type and warns you before you might create a duplicate (e.g. typing "Mike" when "Mike Smith" already exists). It never blocks you, just makes sure you see the match first.

Prospects who convert to real clients are meant to be linked, not duplicated — if someone's already in your outreach pipeline and they sign up, that connection should get made so they stop showing as needing follow-up.

Reassigning clients between trainers ▾

This is always done one client at a time, on purpose — there's no bulk "move everyone from Coach A to Coach B" button. If a trainer leaves, you'll want to thoughtfully distribute their clients across a few different coaches, not dump them all on one person. Reassign a client by editing their profile directly and changing their trainer.

The end-of-day (EOD) system ▾

Each role has their own EOD page tailored to what they actually need to report — a coach's EOD looks different from a GM's. Coaches submit consultations, follow-ups, and check-ins with an AI-assisted "Compose" button that writes a short summary from the real intake data plus their own notes — never invents anything not actually on file.

Community feed content ▾

Industry news posts automatically, refreshed weekly — nothing to do there. Birthdays and gym anniversaries also post automatically, pulled from real client data. The one thing that needs a real person: personal, human content — a new baby, a member's milestone, a shoutout. That goes in through Portal Access Manager's "Community Feed — Personal Post" panel.

Your daily agenda: Coach CRM ▾

This is where your day lives — today's schedule, your client list, notes on each client's account. Start here every shift.

Three intake tools reachable directly from the top bar: Consultation, 6-Wk Follow-Up, Monthly Check-In. Two ways into each: click through from a scheduled appointment on your agenda (pre-fills the client automatically), or open the tool directly and start typing a name — it searches existing clients live and fills in their info the moment you pick a match.

Building & assigning workouts: Gym Floor ▾

Live workout logging with a client in front of you, plus the AI Program Builder for putting together a real program. Sets default to 3 per exercise and are always adjustable — same standard whether you're building it here or a client is scheduling their own routine.

Submitting your EOD ▾

Consultations, 6-week follow-ups, and check-ins you completed that day auto-populate. For each one, the purple ✨ Compose button pulls that appointment's real intake data plus any notes you've already logged about that client from your agenda, and writes a short caption for you — tap it, review it, submit.

Syncing your schedule to your phone ▾

Your Home tab has "Sync to My Phone Calendar" — adds your shifts and coaching appointments as a live calendar. To remove it later: your phone's Calendar app → Settings → find it under your added calendars → unsubscribe. Won't touch anything else on your calendar.

Getting logged in ▾

Your coach sets up your account and gives you a temporary password. Log in with that first — you'll be automatically walked through setting your own password right after, with a code emailed to confirm it's really you.

Home tab ▾

Shows what your coach has planned for you today, if anything. Below that, My Calendar — tap to expand — is entirely yours: any workouts or meals you've scheduled for yourself, days or weeks ahead. Tap any item in it to jump straight into that workout or that recipe, no hunting around.

Training tab ▾

Log a session you just did, or use Schedule Your Own Workouts for the days you're training without your coach — pick a routine (see exactly what's in it first) or build your own from scratch, set a duration, pick which days and how many weeks to repeat.

Nutrition tab ▾

Log your meals, browse the full recipe library in Coach's Table (filters automatically respect anything you've told your coach you can't or won't eat), or ask 🧑‍🍳 Ask the Chef what to make — it knows your real targets and what you've already eaten today. You can also schedule meals in advance the same way you schedule workouts, and a weekly shopping list builds itself from whatever you've planned.

Classes & Progress ▾

Classes: see what's on the schedule and reserve your spot. Progress: your InBody scan history and photos over time, so you can actually see the trend, not just one snapshot.
